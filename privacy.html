Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026. This page explains what information this website collects, why, and what you can ask me to do with it.

Who I am

OpsWithJane is run by Jane Kenda, based in Nairobi, Kenya. I am responsible for the information collected on www.opswithjane.com. You can reach me at opswithjane@gmail.com about anything on this page.

What I collect when you contact me

When you fill in the enquiry form, I collect the details you type in, such as your name, email address, agency name and website, and what you tell me about how you onboard clients. When you book a call, I also get the time you picked and any notes you add.

I use this only to reply to you, prepare for our call and, if we work together, run the project. I do not sell it, rent it or add you to a mailing list without asking.

The form and the booking calendar run on HubSpot, my CRM, which stores your details on its servers in the European Union.

Cookies and analytics

The first time you visit, the site asks whether it can use cookies. If you say no, no analytics cookies are set and nothing below runs. If you say yes, two tools load:

Google Analytics shows me how many people visit, where they come from (for example LinkedIn or Google search), which pages they read and which buttons they click. Google keeps this event data for 2 months.

Microsoft Clarity shows me heatmaps and recordings of how pages are used, such as where people scroll, click or get stuck. Text you type into forms is hidden from recordings, and the enquiry form itself is never recorded. Clarity keeps recordings for 30 days.

I use both only to improve the site. Neither is used for advertising. You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.

Other services the site uses

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs such as IP addresses to deliver pages and keep them secure. Fonts load from Google Fonts. The optional Leak Check quiz runs on Tally, and the answers you give there go to Tally and to me.

How long I keep your details

I keep enquiry and booking details for up to 2 years after we last spoke, unless we work together, in which case I keep project records for as long as the law requires. You can ask me to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You can ask me to show you the information I hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. Email opswithjane@gmail.com and I will reply within 30 days.

If you are unhappy with how I handle your information, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya, or to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes

If I change how the site handles information, I will update this page and the date at the top.
