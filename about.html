About Jane Kenda

I build client onboarding systems so marketing agency owners are no longer the bottleneck between "yes" and delivery.

Why I do this work

At NeuroConnect Africa I watched a founder work constantly and lose sleep over the business. It left me with a belief I still work from: if a business cannot run without its founder, it is not yet a business. The founder is holding it together.

Onboarding is where that usually starts. A client signs, and then the founder is the one sending contracts, chasing logins and briefing the team. I focus on that stretch only, so I can do it properly.

Most people you hire will build what you ask for. I check whether it will work first. I tell you before you spend on it, and I bring a better plan.

My story

I started at the beginning of 2023. Towards the end of that year I was accepted by Salas Staffing as an executive assistant, and that changed my story. My family met the news with doubt. I kept going.

Since then I have worked as an AI tutor, executive assistant, outreach strategist, data analyst, operations director and interim CEO. It looked scattered at the time. Looking back, all of it led here.

General VA work stopped exciting me. What brings me to life is setting up the systems behind operations that run smoothly, so I narrowed down to CRMs and client onboarding workflows for small marketing agencies.

Background

I was Operations and Executive Assistant at NeuroConnect Africa from October 2025 to June 2026. I hold a BSc in Mathematics from the University of Nairobi, and I am HubSpot CRM certified and an ALX Virtual Assistant Programme graduate. I am based in Nairobi, Kenya and work remotely with agencies in the United States.

You will probably enjoy working with OpsWithJane if

  • You run a marketing agency of 1 to 15 people and still onboard new clients by hand.
  • You would rather hear "this won't work yet" before you spend the money than after.
  • You are the one sending contracts, chasing intake forms and briefing your team.
  • You would rather have one onboarding system that works every time than five tools that sort of talk to each other.
  • You want a system your team can run and you fully own.

Probably not a fit if you want a quick patch with no say in how clients are onboarded, or you are not ready to hand off any part of it.
