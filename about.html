About Jane Kenda

I build and maintain the systems that let founder-led businesses run without the founder in every task.

Why I do this work

At NeuroConnect Africa I watched a founder work constantly and lose sleep over the business. It left me with a belief I still work from: if a business cannot run without its founder, it is not yet a business. The founder is holding it together.

Most people you hire will build what you ask for. I check whether it will work first. I notice what others overlook: the automation that looks good but will not hold up, the campaign that will not convert, the idea that never gets off the ground. I tell you before you spend on it, and I bring a better plan.

Background

I was Operations and Executive Assistant at NeuroConnect Africa from October 2025 to June 2026. I hold a BSc in Mathematics from the University of Nairobi, and I am HubSpot CRM certified and an ALX Virtual Assistant Programme and AI Career Essentials graduate. I am based in Nairobi, Kenya and work remotely with clients internationally.

You will probably enjoy working with OpsWithJane if

  • You would rather hear "this won't work yet" before you spend the money than after.
  • You believe your business should run without you in the middle of every task.
  • You are a coach, consultant or boutique agency owner still sending contracts and chasing intake forms yourself.
  • You would rather have one system that works every time than five tools that sort of talk to each other.
  • You value someone who runs and maintains the system after it is built.

Probably not a fit if you want a quick patch with no say in how your client journey works, or you are not ready to hand off any part of it.
