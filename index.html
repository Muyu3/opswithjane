About

The problems
I actually solve.

I work with founders and small teams who are stretched too thin to keep every process running the way it should. Most of the people I support are buried under inbox clutter, missed follow ups, and systems that only exist in someone's head instead of on paper. I come in and build the structure that's missing: clean CRMs, documented workflows, calendars that reflect reality, and trackers that show exactly where things stand at a glance. My mathematics background means precision is my default, so numbers, timelines, and reconciliations get checked twice before they ever reach you. I've supported real estate agents juggling listings, healthcare practices untangling years of scheduling chaos, and coaches drowning in outreach they never had time to organize. If your business runs on you remembering everything, that's exactly what I'm here to fix.
