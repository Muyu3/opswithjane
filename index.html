FAQ

Common questions

What is a client onboarding system?

It is everything that happens between a client saying yes and your team being ready to deliver: intake, contract, payment, welcome, asset and access collection, internal handover and status tracking. I connect these so they run the same way for every client.

Which tools do you build onboarding systems in?

GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Dubsado, Airtable, Asana, ClickUp, Zapier and Make. I build inside the platform you already use, so you own the system.

How long does it take to set up client onboarding automation?

It depends on how many steps and tools are involved. I agree scope and a timeline with you before building anything.

What if my onboarding is messy and undocumented?

That is the usual starting point. The Onboarding Audit and Blueprint maps what happens today, shows where clients stall, and gives you a written plan.

Where are you based, and which time zones work?

I am based in Nairobi, Kenya and work remotely with agencies in the United States. We agree overlap hours at the start.
